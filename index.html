About the firm

Clear advice and careful compliance, with a personal touch

CA Harshit Dudeja is a Chartered Accountant and the founder of Harshit Dudeja & Co., an independent practice based in Delhi NCR. Before starting his own firm, he built his foundation at EY, KPMG, and O.P. Bagla & Co. LLP, working on tax, audit, strategic decision making and compliances for businesses of different sizes.

Today he advises startups, SMEs, large corporates, HNIs, NGOs, and trusts, with a focus on clear, practical advice that holds up under scrutiny.

Chartered Accountant since 2023 · B.Com, Shaheed Bhagat Singh College, University of Delhi · A team of 2 qualified Chartered Accountants and 6+ assistants
